PDS Uploader

PDS Uploader is the private upload tool for Practical Data Science, a statistics and machine-learning teaching channel on YouTube.

It is not a public service, has no sign-up, and serves no third parties. Its only user is the channel owner, uploading the channel's own finished lessons — with their titles, descriptions, tags, custom thumbnails, playlist placement, and scheduled publish times — through the YouTube Data API.

What it does:

  • Uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel
  • Sets custom thumbnails and files videos into the owner's playlists
  • Schedules publication at the channel's regular release times
  • Reads back its own uploads to verify the schedule took effect

What it does not do: it does not read, store, or display any other person's data, comments, or analytics, and it shares data with no one.

See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
