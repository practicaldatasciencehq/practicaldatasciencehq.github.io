Privacy Policy — PDS Uploader

Last updated: October 24, 2026.

PDS Uploader is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the Practical Data Science YouTube channel. It has no public users, no sign-up, and no third-party users.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's own Google OAuth consent, it accesses only that owner's own YouTube channel, for the sole purpose of uploading the owner's own videos: video files and their owner-supplied titles, descriptions, tags, and custom thumbnails, playlist placement, and scheduled publish times. It also reads back the owner's own uploads and playlists to verify an upload succeeded.

What it does not collect

It does not collect, store, or display any other person's data. It does not access comments, analytics, or any other channel's content, and it does not track visitors to this site — this site sets no cookies and collects no analytics.

Storage and sharing

The only data retained are the owner's own video IDs and upload reports, stored locally, and the owner's OAuth credentials, held in a secure credential vault. Data is shared with no one, sold to no one, and used for no purpose other than uploading the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel.

Revocation

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in their Google account security settings; revocation stops all access immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Practical Data Science channel on YouTube.
